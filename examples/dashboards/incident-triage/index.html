Blocked by TraceForce

Denied attempts never become findings

Only Claude Code and Cursor blocks are visible in the lake. Copilot and Cowork blocks, and Cursor prompt blocks, leave no event.

Source: sensitive_data_findings, connector_containment_findings and prompt_injection_findings (daily snapshots of TraceForce's findings), and agent_events for blocks and each incident's activity. An incident is one session's findings. Days are UTC.

Severity, from the finding: critical for credentials and identity or financial data (SSN, card numbers), prompt injections, and destroying company data or infrastructure or disabling device defenses when it ran; high for changes to company systems that ran; medium for changes to the device or dev environment that ran; a risky action that failed drops one step; low for contact details such as email addresses and anything unclassified. An incident takes its most severe finding.

Open means awaiting or under review; resolved is any other status (false positive, acknowledged, won't fix, revoked, used in tests). The matched sensitive value is never in the lake: open the finding in the TraceForce console for evidence.
